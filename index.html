I am a member of the number theory group at King’s. My research concerns the study of conjectures on special values of $L$-functions, such as the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer and Bloch–Kato conjectures, using techniques from Iwasawa theory and the Langlands programme. Much of my work utilises the theory of Euler systems, in particular their behaviour under $p$-adic deformation.
